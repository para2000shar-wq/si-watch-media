SI Watch Uploader – Privacy Policy

SI Watch Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the SI Watch and Kuch Bhi Sunao channels to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels.

It does not collect, store, sell or share any data from other people. The only Google access it uses is permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's own channels, granted by the owner.

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.
